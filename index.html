    Python Frame API on yoda.

    Lua and Fennel. require("dseams").

Call it

Two classifiers

CHILL+

Four-neighbour star. Labels cubic, hexagonal, interfacial, clathrate, and water. It does not assign cage membership.

Cages

Hexagonal cages (HC) are ice Ih. Double-diamond cages (DDC) are ice Ic. Everything else is water on that score.
